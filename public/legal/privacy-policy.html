1. About MiloBroker

MiloBroker helps people start property-related conversations and discover advertised properties, rooms and PG accommodation. This policy explains how the MiloBroker operator handles personal information when you visit our website, use our WhatsApp service or otherwise contact us.

  • Property advertisements: information you submit about a property, such as its description, location, price, photographs and videos. Advertisements may contain information about people or premises.
